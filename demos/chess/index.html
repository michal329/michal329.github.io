Kung Fu Chess · playable browser portSource on GitHub ↗

Chess without turns.

Both sides move at once, pieces take visible travel time, every arrival triggers a cooldown, and a jump destroys any attacker that lands mid-air. A compact JavaScript port of the rules, using the project's own sprite art. The original engine and its websocket multiplayer server are in Python.

You play White. Black moves on its own.
1No turns. Click a white piece, then a highlighted square.
2Travel time. Pieces fly square by square, so you can dodge.
3Jump. Click a selected piece again. An attacker landing on it mid-air dies.
4Win by capturing the king.
